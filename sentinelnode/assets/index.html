Receiver settings

A receiver is a service that accepts Sentinel’s alert messages over HTTP. Use your own receiver or an adapter for your notification provider.

Optional authentication

If your receiver requires a bearer token, set SENTINEL_WEBHOOK_TOKEN before launching Sentinel. The token is kept out of saved data and exports.

Pausing delivery keeps waiting messages. Before changing the receiver, send or cancel those messages.
